Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Ativak Works Studio ("Ativak Works", "we", "us", "our") operates the website https://ativak-works-landing.vercel.app/ (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have over it. By using the Website or contacting us, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you give us directly

  • Name, phone number, email address, and business name or type, when you fill in our contact form, audit request form, or lead form.
  • Messages and project details you share with us on WhatsApp, email, or phone.
  • Any files or screenshots you send us for a project.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, operating system, IP address, pages visited, time spent, and referring website.
  • Approximate location based on IP address.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, as described in Section 5.

c) Advertising and measurement data

Our Meta Pixel and related tools record actions you take on the Website, such as viewing a page, clicking a button, or submitting a form. This helps us measure and improve our Meta and Google advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries, arrange free audits or discovery calls, and provide the services you request.
  • To prepare project quotes, contracts, and invoices.
  • To send service updates related to your project.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Website.
  • To measure the performance of our advertising and to show relevant ads to people who may be interested in our services.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing of Information

We share personal information only in these situations:

  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting (Vercel), analytics, email, WhatsApp communication tools, and cloud services. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Advertising platforms such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google, to measure and optimise our ads.
  • Legal reasons, when we are required by law, court order, or government authority, or to protect our rights and users' safety.
  • Business transfers, if we merge with or are acquired by another company. We will inform you if this happens.

4. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law. Enquiry and project records are normally kept for up to 3 years after the last interaction, unless a longer period is required for legal or accounting reasons.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use the following:

  • Essential cookies: needed for the Website to function.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors use the Website.
  • Advertising cookies and pixels: including the Meta Pixel, which helps us measure ad performance and reach people who may be interested in our services.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the Website works.

6. Third-Party Links

The Website may link to other websites, including social media platforms, WhatsApp, and client projects. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. Please read their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India, you may:

  • Ask us to confirm whether we hold your personal information.
  • Ask for access to, or a copy of, your personal information.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw your consent to processing, where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a complaint with us or with the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when the latest changes were made. Continued use of the Website after changes means you accept the updated policy.
